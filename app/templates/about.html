About NJ Transportation Bids

A free public index of New Jersey transportation construction bids, professional services solicitations, and public notices.

Contact and corrections FAQ and free access Sources and coverage

Browsing is free

You can browse and search the public opportunity boards and follow official source links without an account or payment to this site.

An issuing agency or its procurement platform may require registration or charge for access to documents. Those requirements are set by the official source.

What this index provides

The boards collect notices and anticipated work from public procurement sources so contractors and professional services teams can find relevant opportunities in one place.

Each record links to its source. Available details depend on what that source publishes and what the index can extract.

Use the agency's bid package

This site does not host a complete bid package or accept bids. The issuing agency controls deadlines, addenda, eligibility, pre-bid meetings, and submission instructions.

Open the official source and confirm all requirements before preparing or submitting a response.

Coverage and freshness

Coverage is incomplete. Some procurement sites limit automated access, require registration, or provide information that cannot be reliably extracted. A configured source does not guarantee usable current records.

Source checks follow different schedules, including daily and weekly checks. A site update time does not mean every source or deadline was verified at that moment. Review source coverage and last-check times, and confirm the current notice at the official source.

Forecasts describe anticipated work and can change or pass their expected window without a new solicitation. Missing, conflicting, or uncertain dates require confirmation with the issuing agency.
